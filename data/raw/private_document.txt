Zainab Chishti was born in Gujranwala in 1944. She studied at Harrowgate University and later at Alderwick College of Science. She received the Kestrel Prize in Physics in 1988. Colleagues usually referred to her simply as "Dr. Chishti". In 1978, Dr. Chishti joined Ashdown Institute of Physics. Zainab Chishti's doctoral advisor was Salman Siddiqui. He later moved to Calder College of Science. Despite a persistent rumour, she never studied at Northgate Institute of Physics.
